The Three Phases

Every co-founder relationship moves through these stages. Most teams don't realize it until something breaks.

01

Forged Under Pressure

The foundation isn't built through exercises. It's built in the trenches—shipping, fundraising, hiring, firing. This is where you find out if what you talked about actually works when everything's on fire.

02

Scaling & Strain

Growth amplifies everything. The cracks you ignored at seed become canyons at Series B. If it breaks here, go back to phase one.

03

Success & Preservation

The danger zone. Money brings ego, outside forces, and new pressure. This is where partnerships that survived scaling still fall apart.

You can't get to phase three without solving one and two first.
